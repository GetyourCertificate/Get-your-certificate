About This Course

Manual handling is an important part of many workplaces. This course introduces learners to the principles of safe manual handling and helps them understand common workplace risks.

You will learn how to identify hazards, understand safe lifting principles and reduce the risk of manual handling injuries.

What You Will Learn

  • ✓ Understand what manual handling means
  • ✓ Identify common manual handling risks
  • ✓ Understand common types of injuries
  • ✓ Learn basic safe lifting principles
  • ✓ Understand risk assessment principles
  • ✓ Apply safer manual handling practices
